Business customer user journey

flowchart TD
    Start([New Customer]) --> Step1[1. Collect Product Information]
    Step1 --> IK[Industry Knowledge Agent]
    IK --> Step2[2. Define Goals]
    Step2 --> BR[Branding & Strategy Agent]
    BR --> Step3[3. Create Initial Videos]
    Step3 --> I2V[Image-to-Video Agent]
    I2V --> Step4[4. Schedule and Upload]
    Step4 --> MM[Media Management Agent]
    MM --> Step5[5. Show Centralized Data]
    Step5 --> AN[Analytics Agent]
    AN --> Continuous[Continuous Engagement]
    Continuous --> IK
    Continuous --> CE[Customer Engagement Agent]
    Continuous --> AN
    Continuous --> I2V
Flow: The orchestration layer holds brand id, product id, campaign id, and target channel. It calls agents in sequence (e.g. industry knowledge → image-to-video → media management) and passes a minimal context object. All agents read from and write back to the product/brand context.

User Journey: New customers go through 5 steps: (1) Collect product information via Industry Knowledge Agent, (2) Define goals with Branding & Strategy Agent, (3) Create videos with Image-to-Video Agent, (4) Schedule uploads with Media Management Agent, and (5) View analytics. After setup, agents work continuously for ongoing engagement.
