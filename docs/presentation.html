Core agents — what the system does for you

Six agents that handle industry knowledge, creatives, distribution, engagement, analytics, and store listings.

Industry knowledge

  • Best-practice library by industry (e.g. home goods, apparel, beauty)
  • U.S. compliance (FTC, platform policies, do/don’t by vertical)
  • Stable output for other agents; optional real-time trends & recommendations
  • Daily newsletter: platform rules, regulations, trends, competitor updates
  • Quick research for new customers: web search & knowledge base creation
  • Customer-specific knowledge storage for personalized recommendations

Image-to-video

  • Commercial and UGC-style videos from your assets
  • Industry knowledge integration: daily updates from Industry Knowledge Agent
  • Video prompt template library: product showcase, influencer style, commercials
  • Prompt engineering updates for each video LLM (Runway, Pika, etc.)
  • Input sources: industry knowledge, video requirements, product photos
  • Brand guidelines (logo, colors, style); pre-publish checks
  • Emotion tuning; learns from performance; generation caps per campaign

Media management

  • Create and manage scheduling of creatives generation
  • Automated upload to social platforms with pre-determined schedules
  • Schedule and publish creatives to Meta, TikTok, then more
  • Per-platform sizing; content calendar; failed-job handling
  • A/B testing and optimization from engagement data

Customer engagement

  • Social media response: reply to comments and messages
  • Lead consolidation: consolidate sales leads back to brand owner
  • Draft replies for approval; tone and escalation rules

Analytics

Store / listing management

Business customer user journey

Step-by-step process for onboarding new business customers and how each agent contributes to their success.

1 Collect product information

Industry Knowledge Agent stores customer requirements, performs web search, parses website information, and creates initial industry knowledge base.

2 Define goals

Branding & Strategy Agent inputs brand information, searches industry knowledge and competitors, and creates a social media plan based on goals.

3 Create initial videos

Image-to-Video Agent collects information from Industry Knowledge Agent daily and creates videos from product photos.

4 Schedule and upload

Media Management Agent executes scheduled uploads based on the plan from Branding & Strategy Agent.

5 Show centralized data

Analytics Agent generates live dashboard, provides insights and recommendations, and alerts on key metrics changes.

Continuous engagement

After initial setup, agents work continuously:

  • Industry Knowledge Agent: Daily updates and newsletter distribution
  • Customer Engagement Agent: Real-time response to comments and messages, lead consolidation
  • Analytics Agent: Continuous monitoring and alerting
  • Image-to-Video Agent: Regular video generation based on performance and trends
